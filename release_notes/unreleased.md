### Interactive usage chart

- **Pan through history** — drag the chart (or use a horizontal wheel/trackpad swipe) to scroll back through older buckets; double-click or hit the **Latest →** button to jump back to now.
- **Hover details** — a highlight band follows the pointer with a gliding tooltip card showing input/output tokens for each bucket.
- **Readable axes** — reference lines at max and half-max, and axis ticks that adapt to the panel width (two-line labels in hourly view).
- **Smoother motion** — bars grow in with a staggered animation, and switching granularity zooms in/out instead of snapping.

### Small touches

- The quit button now uses the power glyph instead of an X.
